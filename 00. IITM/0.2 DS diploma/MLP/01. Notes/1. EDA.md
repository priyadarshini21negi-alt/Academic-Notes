### Q. Drop all the samples(rows) with missing values strictly greater than 2. How many samples remains after that ? 
`new_df = df.dropna(thresh=df.shape[1]-2`
- `df.shape[1]` gives the total number of columns. 
- subtracting 2 gives the minimum number of non-missing values required
- `thresh` keeps rows with at least that many non-missing values.

`new_df = df[df.isna().sum(axis=1) <= 2]`

### Q. Drop all the samples(rows) with missing values in the original dataframe. How many samples remains after that ?
`df.dropna().shape[0]`
- `shape[0]` returns the number of remaining rows (samples).
- `df.dropna()` removes every row containing at least one missing value (`NaN`).
