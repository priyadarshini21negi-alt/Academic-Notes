
# Role of synapses in facilitating these networks: 
## Synapse - 
The places where neurons connect and communicate with each other are called synapses.
Specialized junctions that allow communication between neurons in the nervous system. These connections are essential for transmitting information and signals within the brain and throughout the body.

Each neuron has anywhere between a few to hundreds of thousands of synaptic connections, and these connections can be with itself, neighboring neurons, or neurons in other regions of the brain.

## Types of Synapses
1. **Chemical Synapses**
	- most common type
	- involve release and reception of *neurotransmitters*,  which are chemical messengers that transmit signals
