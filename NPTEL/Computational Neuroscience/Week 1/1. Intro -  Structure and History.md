# Neuron Structure 

<img src="neuron.png" style="float: left; width: 320px; margin-right: 20px;">consists of a cell soma (body), dendrites, and an axon. 
The **soma** contains the nucleus and is responsible for maintaining the cell. 
The **dendrites** are protrusions that extend from the soma in multiple directions, while the axon is a thin structure that serves as the neuron's output. 

The axon can connect with multiple neurons, forming **synapses**, the points at which neurons connect to each other.
A **synapse** is the **junction where one neuron communicates with another neuron or with another cell**.

# History
- concept of the neuron as a distinct unit was established by the Spanish pathologist Santiago Ramón y Cajal.
- #### Theories of Camilo Golgi and Santiago Ramón y Cajal. 
	- Golgi proposed the reticular theory, suggesting that the nervous system was network-like, similar to the circulatory system. 
	- Cajal proposed the neuron doctrine, suggesting that the neuron is a single entity and that neurons are connected in a discontinuous manner. 
	- neuron doctrine was later confirmed with the advent of electron microscopy.
