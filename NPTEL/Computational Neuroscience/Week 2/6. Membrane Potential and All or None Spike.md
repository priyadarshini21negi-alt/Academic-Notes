## Questions 
### Definition and Quantitative Meaning (Resting state)
#### Q. What does a resting potential of $-70\text{ mV}$ physically represent in terms of charge distribution across the membrane, and what maintains it?
Inside the neuron is $70\text{ mV}$ more negative than the outside. 
This is maintained by $\text{Na}^+/\text{K}^+$ pumps and ion channels, alongside trapped intracellular negative proteins.

### Mechanism of Depolarization (Action Potential Generation)
#### Q. What exact ionic mechanism causes rapid depolarization once the threshold potential is reached?
Reaching threshold opens **voltage-gated $\text{Na}^+$ channels**, allowing a rapid **influx of $\text{Na}^+$** down its concentration gradient, which makes the inside of the cell positively charged (depolarization). 

### Process & Propagation (Signal Transmission)
#### Q. How is an electrical signal propagated along an axon and converted into communication at the synapse? 
The action potential propagates as a **wave of depolarization** down the axon, triggering the **release of neurotransmitters** into the synaptic cleft to signal the next neuron.
